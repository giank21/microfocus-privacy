Privacy policy — MicroFocus AI

Last updated: 19 September 2026

MicroFocus AI (“the app”) is a productivity widget for Android. It is built offline-first. Routine titles, completion history, places you save, and the current focus suggestion are stored only on your device.

Data we process on your device

  • Micro-tasks, routines, streaks, quiet history and theme preference (local database)
  • Optional places (home, work, gym, study) as a latitude/longitude pair used exclusively to register a 150 m geofence on the device
  • Optional notification permission for gentle reminders, window-edge alerts and an optional daily digest
  • A locally encrypted flag that remembers whether Google Play reports an active Pro subscription

We do not create an account. We do not operate a profile server. We do not sell data. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not include third-party analytics SDKs.

Data processed by others

  • Google Play Billing processes the purchase of MicroFocus Pro (monthly or yearly). Google’s payments privacy policy applies to card data. We never see your full payment details.
  • RevenueCat (if configured) validates Play receipts so Pro can be restored. It receives Play purchase tokens, not your location or task list.

Location

Location is optional. If you save a place, the app registers a low-power geofence (including background geofence callbacks so the widget can update when you arrive). Location coordinates are not uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes local places. You can revoke location in Android Settings at any time; time-of-day suggestions keep working.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. Reminder content is generated on-device from your local routines. We do not send push from a remote messaging server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Retention and deletion

All local data is removed when you uninstall the app. Subscription status remains with Google Play until you cancel in the Play Store subscriptions page. You can restore purchases after reinstall if your Google account still holds an active subscription.
